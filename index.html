About this page

A first-person view from inside Sigil, the City of Doors: a ring-shaped city built on the inside of a torus about twenty miles around, with no sky, just a sourceless light that brightens and dims. Look straight up and you see the far side of the city across the ring.

The streets, buildings and landmark designs are generated by code on your machine. Ward and landmark placement follows the setting's lore; no official maps or artwork were used.

Controls: drag to look around, wheel or pinch to zoom, arrow keys to turn. Click the map (or a landmark on it) to travel. The gear button changes eye height, light and quality.
